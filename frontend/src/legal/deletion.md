# **TechWatch** USER DATA DELETION INSTRUCTIONS

**Last Updated: 26.02.2026**

At **TechWatch**, we respect your privacy and provide you with full control over your personal data. In compliance with data protection regulations and platform policies (including Facebook and Google), we provide the following instructions on how to request the deletion of your user data.

---

## How to Request Data Deletion

To protect our users' security and prevent unauthorized deletion requests, we require proof of identity before processing any data removal.

### Step 1: Submit Your Request
Send an email to: **info@techwatch.app** with the subject line: **"Data Deletion Request"**.

### Step 2: Proof of Identity
To verify that you are the rightful owner of the account, you must:
1.  **Send the request from the same email address** that you used to sign in or register for **TechWatch**. 
2.  If you no longer have access to that email, you must provide additional information to verify your identity (such as a unique User ID or details of your last activity within the app).

*Requests sent from an unrelated email address without further proof of identity will be rejected for security reasons.*

### Step 3: Provide Details
In the body of the email, please include:
* Your **Full Name**
* The **Email Address** associated with the account you wish to delete.

---

## What Happens After You Submit a Request?

* **Verification:** We will first verify your identity based on the email provided.
* **Processing Time:** Once verified, we will process the deletion within **30 days**.
* **Scope of Deletion:** We will permanently delete your profile information and usage history from our active databases.
* **Confirmation:** We will send a confirmation email once the deletion process is complete.

## Third-Party App Permissions
If you signed in using Google or Facebook, we also recommend revoking access directly in their security settings:
* **Google:** [Google Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
* **Facebook:** **Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites**
